This M.S. independent research project uses U.S. bank-level data from WRDS to study how quantitative easing relates to bank risk-taking. I apply propensity-score matching combined with difference-in-differences (PSM–DID) and highlight heterogeneous effects across banks with different mortgage-backed securities exposures. Results point to increased risk-taking following QE1, with implications for financial stability and regulation.

**Supervisors:** Jeffrey Smith, Matthew Wiswall, and Charles Engel (University of Wisconsin–Madison).  
**Period:** Jul. 2021 – Nov. 2021.
