Using large-scale cross-sectional survey data, I analyze logistic regression and machine-learning models to identify socioeconomic predictors of telehealth utilization for ADHD treatment among U.S. adults (18+). The analysis compares traditional methods with ML to capture patterns relevant for equitable healthcare policy. A final SVM specification illustrates nonlinear structure in the data; principal component analysis summarizes covariation across social determinants of health.
