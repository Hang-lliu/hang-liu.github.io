We construct ZIP-code-level panel data for California combining traffic speed, vehicle registration, and transit ridership. Using two-way fixed effects and county-by-year fixed effects, we estimate how battery electric vehicle (BEV) adoption relates to congestion; doubling BEV adoption is associated with roughly a 1.2% reduction in peak-hour traffic speed. We discuss mechanisms including higher vehicle ownership, increased travel demand, and reduced public transit ridership.
