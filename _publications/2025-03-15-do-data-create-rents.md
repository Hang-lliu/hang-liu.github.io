This paper studies how data-sharing platforms affect market power and pricing in rental housing markets. We construct granular panel data at the apartment–ZIP-code–time level and exploit the RealPage–LRO merger and COVID-era stimulus as quasi-exogenous shocks in an event-study framework. We document that platform dominance is associated with higher rental markups and stronger price responses to demand shocks.

**Co-author:** Rodney Ramcharan (USC).
