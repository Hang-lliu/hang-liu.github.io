I am a Ph.D. student in Economics at the [University of Southern California](https://dornsife.usc.edu/econ/). My work focuses on empirical applications—using microdata and modern econometric tools to study macroeconomic and finance-related questions.

Prior to USC, I completed graduate training in econometrics and economics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison and The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, and received my B.S. in Economics from Huazhong University of Science and Technology.
